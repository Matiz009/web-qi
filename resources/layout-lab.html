Lesson 1 · The Box Model

Every element on a page is a rectangle made of four layers. From the inside out: content, padding, border and margin. The ruler on the stage measures real pixels, so you can check the maths yourself.

Key idea

By default, width only measures the content. Padding and border are added on top. box-sizing: border-box changes that.

    Lesson 2 · Flexbox
    
        
    Flexbox lays items out in one direction at a time. The direction you choose is the main axis; the one across it is the cross axis. Container properties arrange all items. Item properties change one item. Click an item to select it.
    
      
    
      
    Key idea
    justify-content works on the main axis. align-items works on the cross axis. Change flex-direction and watch them swap.

      Lesson 3 · CSS Grid
      
        
      Grid lays items out in two directions at once: columns and rows. You define the tracks, and items drop into the cells. The red dashed lines are grid lines; their numbers are what you use with grid-column and grid-row.
      
      
      
      
      Key idea
      1fr means one share of the free space. repeat(3, 1fr) makes three equal columns that resize with the container.
